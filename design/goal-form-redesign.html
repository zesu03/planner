New goal — form options
Three ways to finish the New-goal form, drawn to scale. All of them fix the concrete issues: the doubled “New goal” heading, edge-to-edge inputs, and the preview that only appears after typing. Pick one (or mix) and I’ll build it alongside the portfolio-grid list.
Option A · recommended
Compose — two columns with a living preview
Fields on the left in a readable column; on the right, a preview card rendered exactly like the portfolio-grid card, always visible and updating as you type. It ties the form to the list you just picked and uses the desktop width. On mobile the preview moves to the top and the fields stack beneath.
