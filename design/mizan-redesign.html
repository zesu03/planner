Mizan — redesign options
Mizan is already well-organised (decluttered, spiritual-first, productivity behind collapsibles). The one real weakness is the tall single column of full-width cards that wastes desktop width. Three ways to fix it. Content is representative.
Option 1 · recommended
Dashboard grid — hero + paired cards
A stronger “This week” hero (prayer-rate ring + the digest as a scannable stat grid), then a responsive 2-column grid: wide cards (Prayer Health, Qaza) span full width; medium cards (Voluntary + Habit Health, Patterns) pair up. Productivity stays behind collapsibles at the bottom. Fills the width; reads as a composed dashboard, not a scroll.

Sectioned sub-nav
