Muhasaba — redesign options
Three ways to hold the nightly reckoning. Content is representative (pillars shown abbreviated — every current field stays). The goal: keep the reverence, cut the long-scroll wall, and give the ritual a sense of place and progress.
Option 1 · recommended
Reckoning workspace — reflection + a “Tonight” rail
The reflection flows down the main column; a sticky right rail holds the day’s facts (streak, prayers, focus, how much of tonight you’ve done) and the Mirror. Context sits beside the ritual instead of scattered through it, and the desktop width finally earns its keep. On mobile the rail folds to a compact summary card on top.

Option 2
Guided ladder — refined single column

Option 3
Stepper — one pillar at a time
The reckoning as a paced ritual: a progress rail across the top, then one pillar filling the frame at a time (Back / Next), ending in Du‘a + the Mirror. Most reverent and focused — you sit with one question before moving on. Trade-off: reviewing or quickly editing a past day takes more taps (mitigated by letting you jump via the step dots).
